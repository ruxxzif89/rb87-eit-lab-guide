Free-space laser source in 3D: direct, or through a PM fibre

The Yanban RB-795-DFB-BFW is a free-space butterfly laser with no isolator inside. Our setup (decided 19 Oct 2026) feeds the bench through a PM fibre; the direct way is shown as the fallback. Switch between them, step through the alignment, and read the data-sheet numbers and the budgets on the right. The part shapes are the symbolic models of the equipment list, at the 100 mm beam height on a 25 mm grid; the positions are an example layout. The beam is drawn three times wider than it is; its sizes in the tables are computed from the data sheets.
