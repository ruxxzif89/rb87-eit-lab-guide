1 · Laser, isolation and frequency lock
L01795 nm single-frequency DFB laser, PM-fibre pigtailed, with built-in micro-isolator (14-pin butterfly)1 pcToptica Eagleyard EYP-DFB-0795-00015-1500-BFY12-0005: 5–15 mW from the PM fibre, 0.6–1 MHz linewidth, FC/APC (narrow key), PER 20 dB. Not the BFW11 type, which is a free-space collimated output without a fibre
EYP-DFB-0795-00015-1500-BFY12-0005		The single source for the control, the probe and the SAS reference. A DFB is narrow (~MHz), mode-hop free over its current range and tunes with current/temperature, which is exactly what the lock needs. The fibre pigtail removes the laser-to-fibre alignment, and the micro-isolator inside the module is a first line of defence. Up to 15 mW: do a power budget (isolator, AOM double-pass, polarization optics) before ordering; the vendor can quote a higher-power pigtailed version.gophotonics.com ↗planned
L0214-pin butterfly laser mount with TEC and thermistor connections1 pcThorlabs LM14S2 (butterfly mount), matched to the laser pin-out
LM14S2		Holds the butterfly package, makes the electrical connections to the controller and conducts heat. Without a proper mount the TEC cannot stabilize the chip and the wavelength drifts.Thorlabs search ↗to procure
L03Laser diode current source + TEC temperature controller with analog modulation input1 pcThorlabs ITC502 benchtop controller: 0–200 mA laser current (noise < 1.5 µA rms, 10 Hz–10 MHz; compliance > 6 V), TEC ±2 A / 16 W, thermistor input (10 Ω–19.9 kΩ), analog current modulation 20 mA/V with 0–500 kHz bandwidth
ITC502		Drives the DFB with low-noise current and holds its temperature. Its modulation input is the actuator of the frequency lock (the lock box adds a correction to the current) and carries the dither for lock-in detection. Current noise here becomes laser frequency noise (about 1.4 GHz per mA), so < 1.5 µA rms is about 2 MHz. Set its current limit to 170 mA or lower before connecting the laser (maximum rating 170 mA, normal drive 160 mA). The modulation coefficient is 20 mA/V, about 28 GHz per volt: attenuate the lock-box output about 100:1 into the 10 kΩ input. The laser reads about 20 kΩ at 10 °C, so run the chip at 15 °C or warmer to stay inside the thermistor range.Thorlabs search ↗in the lab
L04Digital lock box (error-signal generation + PI/PID servo)1 pcRed Pitaya STEMlab 125-14 running the open-source Linien / PyRPL laser-lock software
STEMlab 125-14		Turns the SAS photodiode signal into an error signal (lock-in at the dither frequency) and closes the loop onto the laser controller. A programmable box is much cheaper than a commercial servo and gives slope-sign, gain and integrator control. Upgrade path: Vescent D2-125.redpitaya.com ↗to procure
L05Fibre collimator, FC/APC, f ≈ 11 mm (≈1.5 mm beam)1 pcThorlabs CFC11A-B (FC/APC, 650–1050 nm). It follows the fibre isolator: match the APC connector and clock the key for the polarization you want.
CFC11A-B		Turns the fibre output into a collimated ~1.5 mm beam, the size the AOM aperture and cell need. A mismatched connector (PC vs APC) damages the ferrule or causes back-reflection into the laser.Thorlabs search ↗planned
L06Fibre-based dual-stage optical isolator, 795 nm, PM fibre, FC/APC in and out1 pcDK Photonics 780–1100 nm TGG-based dual-stage isolator (PM780-HP fibre, FC/APC): isolation min 40 dB, typically 50–55 dB, insertion loss about 1 dB typ (1.5 dB max). Alternative: Agiltron PM780 dual-stage (> 45 dB, < 2.5 dB; confirm it is offered at 795 nm). Thorlabs IO-J-780APC is single-stage, 33–37 dB, 770–790 nm only: not suitable
DK Photonics 780~1100nm dual-stage (PM780-HP, FC/APC)		Replaces the free-space isolator. It sits in the fibre between the laser and the collimator, so there is nothing to align, no free-space loss and no extra beam height to match, and the light stays isolated until the collimator. It stops reflections from the AOM double-passes and cell windows from re-entering the DFB (even 10⁻⁴ feedback can cause mode hops) and adds to the micro-isolator inside the laser. Catalogue dual-stage fibre isolators reach 40–55 dB at this wavelength; we found no catalogue 60 dB part, so ask the vendor for a quote and a test report.findlight.net ↗to procure

GT10-B		Removes the strong control after the cell far better than a PBS (10⁵ vs 10³), leaving only the probe on the detector. Without it the control leaks onto the photodiode and hides the EIT peak.Thorlabs search ↗to procure
O16Rotation mount for the Glan–Taylor1 pcThorlabs RSP1X15/M or PRM1/M
RSP1X15/M		Sets the transmission axis to the probe polarization and to maximum extinction.Thorlabs search ↗to procure

3080-125		One per arm. 4 · SAS reference
S01Rb reference cell for SAS, quartz Ø19 × 75 mm, natural Rb, no buffer gas, room temperature1 pcPrecision Glassblowing stock cell AB-RB-Q-UV-19X75-AW (quartz, 19 mm OD, 75 mm path, natural Rb, no buffer gas, uncoated windows), $475 on the vendor page
AB-RB-Q-UV-19X75-AW		Provides the sub-Doppler lines the laser is locked to. 5 · Rb cell, heating, magnetic field and shielding
C01Quartz ⁸⁷Rb vapour cell with 10 Torr neon, Ø25 mm, 25 mm vapour path (about 28 mm long with two 1.6 mm windows), uncoated, custom1 pcPrecision Glassblowing custom quartz cell with the geometry and fill of the published Zeeman-EIT cell (DeRose et al. 2023, which is Pyrex): Ø25 mm, 25 mm vapour path, enriched ⁸⁷Rb, 10 Torr Ne, windows uncoated. Needs a quote: the stock Ø19 × 75 mm cell at $475 is only a price reference
custom (quote)		The EIT medium, copying the published cell to avoid errors: 25 mm path, pure ⁸⁷Rb (no absorption by ⁸⁵Rb) and 10 Torr neon, which slows atoms diffusing out of the beam and gives Zeeman-EIT lines a few kHz wide. The paper runs it at 55-65 °C with a 50 mG field inside a shield that keeps stray fields below 0.2 mG. Price is our estimate for a custom cell until the vendor quotes.precisionglassblowing.com ↗planned

KHLV-103/10		Warms the cell to ~60 °C (OD ≈ 10). Bifilar winding cancels the heater’s own magnetic field, which would otherwise broaden the Zeeman resonance.omega.com ↗planned

C04Temperature controller for the cell heater1 pcThorlabs TC300B

Critical-path items Without the µ-metal shield and solenoid, Zeeman EIT cannot be made narrow or reproducible. Without the laser controller and lock box, the laser cannot be locked (Milestone 1). Schedule these first.
